iPhone 16 / What It Contains

The ingredients.

Listed by component. All quantities approximate. All origins filler.

Cobalt

Kolwezi, DRC — Battery cathode — ≈1.12g

The lithium-ion cell requires cobalt to hold its charge. It arrives as cobalt hydroxide, refined from ore excavated by hand in the Lualaba province. Some of the hands belong to children.

The battery runs for 27 hours. The mine runs indefinitely.

Tantalum

Eastern DRC — Logic board capacitors — ~200 units

Derived from coltan, a grey-black mineral found in abundance in eastern Congo. Control of coltan mines has financed armed conflict for decades. Each phone contains roughly 200 capacitors, each containing a trace of this history.

Present in every modern smartphone. Rarely disclosed.

Tin

Bangka Island, Indonesia — Solder throughout

The solder that holds the board together is mostly tin. On Bangka Island, miners dig into unstable coastal pits and nearshore seabeds. Collapse is common. Disclosure is not.

Thousands of kilometers from the nearest Apple Store.

Rare Earths

Inner Mongolia, China — Camera lens and speaker magnets

Neodymium, dysprosium, and praseodymium stabilize the camera and power the earpiece. Separation of rare earth elements produces radioactive tailings. Inner Mongolia processes most of the world's supply.

Beautiful photographs. Difficult waste streams.
