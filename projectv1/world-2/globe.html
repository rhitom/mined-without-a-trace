iPhone 16 / Where It Comes From

A map of origins.

Cobalt route. Each dot is a place. Each place is a story not printed on the box.

Kolwezi, DRC
Shenzhen, China
Bangka, Indonesia
Cupertino, US

  • Kolwezi, Democratic Republic of Congo

    Cobalt — Tantalum — Gold

    The Lualaba province holds some of the largest cobalt deposits on earth. Artisanal miners — some as young as six — extract ore by hand in unregulated pits. Much of it eventually enters the battery in your pocket.

  • Bangka Island, Indonesia

    Tin

    Nearshore tin mining has destroyed the island's coral reefs and fishing economy. The men who mine it sell it by the kilo. It becomes solder. It becomes the joints holding your logic board together.

  • Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region, China

    Rare Earth Elements

    The separation plant at Bayan Obo processes rare earth ore into the powders that become magnets, phosphors, and stabilizers. The tailings pond is one of the largest radioactive waste sites in the world.

  • Cupertino, California

    Design — Distribution

    The object is designed here. It is not made here. It arrives, already assembled, already clean, already sealed in glass.
