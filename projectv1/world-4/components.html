① — CAMERA RING

Lens Assembly

Neodymium (Nd) · Rare Earth

Optical image stabilization magnet. Sourced predominantly from Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region, China. Separation from ore produces radioactive waste streams.

② — LOGIC BOARD

Capacitor Array (~200 units)

Tantalum (Ta) · Conflict Mineral

Derived from coltan ore. Eastern DRC supply routes have historically financed armed groups. Present in every modern smartphone logic board.

③ — BATTERY

Lithium-Ion Cell

Cobalt (Co) · ≈1.12g per unit

Cathode material. Over 70% of global cobalt originates in the Democratic Republic of Congo. Artisanal mining involves an estimated 40,000 child laborers.

④ — TAPTIC ENGINE

Vibration Actuator Mass

Tungsten (W) · High Density

Weighted mass in haptic feedback mechanism. Bolivia and China are primary sources. Supply chain less documented than 3TG minerals.
